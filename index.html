How the ranking works

Each fixture's probability comes from real bookmaker odds, de-vigged (the bookmaker's margin removed) by dividing the Over side's implied probability by the combined Over + Under implied probability, then taking the median across every book that posts a price. Fixtures with no usable line for that market are left out entirely — never estimated or guessed.

Install as an app

Add Kuuku's Odds to your home screen for one-tap access, no browser tabs.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share icon in the toolbar
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen)
  3. Confirm by tapping Install
